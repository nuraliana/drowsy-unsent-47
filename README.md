An entry for the make.fun bounty **"Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint"** — one
message that was typed and never sent.

> To: the woman in the flat below mine
>
> I still turn my music down at 9 p.m. even though you've been gone a year,
> because the night you banged on the ceiling I finally slept through till
> morning, and I've never told you that your anger was the only thing that
> ever made me feel watched over.

Run `main.py` to write the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
See `CANDIDATES.md` for the eight candidates considered and how they scored.

The entry is just two lines: a `To:` line naming the woman in the flat below mine,
and the confession itself — one line, typed and never sent.
